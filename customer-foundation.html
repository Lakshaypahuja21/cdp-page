Source Integration: Every Customer System in One Inventory

Source Integration starts with an inventory of every system that holds customer data: store tills, web orders, the email platform, the loyalty app and consent records. Each source is documented with its owner, its refresh schedule and the identifiers it carries, such as email address, phone number or loyalty ID. The inventory is the map that every later rule refers to.

Source Integration

Identity Resolution: Match Rules Written Down and Approved

Identity Resolution joins records through a match policy that fits on one page: which identifiers are trusted, in what order they apply, and which combinations must never merge, such as shared family email addresses. Business owners approve the policy. Uncertain matches go to a review queue, and a person decides before any records merge.

Identity Resolution

Customer Record Governance: One Record, Owned and Measured

Customer Record Governance sets which source wins when values conflict, who owns each field and how changes are logged. The share of customers matched across systems, the number of duplicate records and the number left unmatched are tracked every week, so a drop is visible when it happens. Every merge can be traced back to the rule that caused it.
