Source and Identity Review: What Is Collected and How It Joins

The review begins with a list of every customer data source, its identifiers and its consent records. The share of customers matched across systems and the number of duplicate records are measured on live data, without changing anything. The output shows which sources join cleanly, which join poorly and which customers cannot be reached for lack of consent.

Source and Identity Review

Quality and Maturity Scoring: Every Score Linked to Evidence

Quality and Maturity Scoring rates data quality, identity, definitions, activation and governance on one scale. AI agents run the repetitive checks, such as missing values, duplicate records and out-of-date feeds. Each score links to the query or record that supports it, so any finding can be checked.

Quality and Maturity Scoring

Findings and Roadmap: Signed Off by a Consultant

Every finding is reviewed and signed off by a consultant before it reaches the report. The roadmap ranks fixes by effect and effort. The next step is the client’s choice: act on the plan in-house, or ask our team to carry out the work and keep the platform healthy.
