Audience Building: Defined Once in Business Terms

Audience Building turns a business request, such as best customers who have gone quiet, into a saved rule on the customer record. Marketers create and change audiences without an engineering ticket. Each audience has a named owner, a version history and a size that is checked before it is sent.

Audience Building

Consent and Suppression: Opt-outs Reach Every Tool

Consent and Suppression store each customer’s permissions on the customer record, by channel. An opt-out in one tool is applied to every audience and every destination on the next sync. Suppression lists, such as recent purchasers or complaints, are applied in the same step, so no audience leaves the platform unchecked.

Consent and Suppression

Channel Delivery and Feedback: Changes Sent, Results Returned

Channel Delivery sends audiences to email, advertising platforms and store outreach lists. After the first load, only changed records are sent, which keeps volumes and platform costs down. Purchase and conversion events return to the ad platforms, and audience membership is logged so a campaign can be reproduced later.

Channel Delivery

What marketing teams do with it

Four outcomes that follow once audiences, consent and delivery sit on one customer record.

Win back customers before they lapse

Customers whose activity is slowing are found early and reached with one offer across email, SMS and social.

Stop paying to acquire existing customers

Known buyers are excluded from acquisition audiences, so the budget goes to people who have not bought yet.

Give ad platforms real revenue

Purchases return with their value and a customer match, so campaigns optimise towards sales and not clicks.

Personalise with full context

Browse and basket journeys take account of what the customer already owns and has agreed to receive.
